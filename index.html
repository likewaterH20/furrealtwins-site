Day one.
Then the internet.

A startup cat brand that went viral on its very first post — and then had to figure out what to do next.

We posted once. It went viral the same day. Everything since has been us trying to keep up with two cats who never asked for an audience.

The plan was simple: build a real IP out of real personalities. Rey and Admiral Akbar aren't invented characters — they're our cats, and every trait on screen is one they actually have. Admiral really does judge from across the room. Rey really does get there first.

Every episode of Rey & Admiral Akbar starts as something that genuinely happened in our apartment. The zoomies at midnight. The stare at 3am. The war over one warm spot on the couch. We exaggerate the timing, not the personalities.

We're small, we're new, and the merch only exists because people asked for it. That's why it unlocks as we grow instead of all at once — we'd rather make less of it and make it right.
